# Biological-audio attribution and redistribution notes

Scope: **Pre-M5 development PoC only**. The original recordings remain
third-party works under the licenses below. This repository does not relicense
them, and neither original nor transformed audio binaries are committed.

The local development derivatives decode one frozen three-second interval,
mix stereo channels by the documented arithmetic-mean rule where needed, and
resample to 32 kHz mono float32 without normalization or other signal
processing. Anyone redistributing a derivative must retain the applicable
attribution and license terms. In particular, the CC BY-SA recordings retain
their attribution and ShareAlike obligations.
